## Purpose

Use this template after watching classmates' 5-minute group book review videos.

Your goals are to:

- Show that you watched and understood each presentation
- Practice constructive academic feedback
- Connect classmates' books to broader course themes
- Reflect on group contribution in a fair and specific way

Peer review responses are graded individually. Complete reviews for **3 different group videos**.

I will share the anonymized peer review responses with each group after the assignment is due.

---

## Peer Review Participation Criteria

Peer review participation is worth **10 points**:

- Completion: 4 points
- Quality of insight: 3 points
- Meaningful course connection: 3 points

---

## Guidelines for Constructive Feedback

Strong peer feedback should be:

- Specific, with references to ideas from the video
- Focused on the group's explanation, analysis, and presentation choices
- Connected to course themes such as history, representation, ethics, labor, technology, serious games, indie vs. AAA development, or community
- Respectful and useful for revision or reflection

Avoid vague comments such as "good job" or "it was confusing" without explaining what made it effective or unclear.
