# INT/AME 264 / Module Guide

Week 06 / Gaming's Impact on People and Society

## Games, Society, Religion, War, and Ethics

Exploring the impact of video games on civic engagement and the portrayal of war and violence.

This week, we explore how video games influence civic engagement and discuss the representation of war and violence in gaming. We will analyze the social and political implications of these themes and their effects on players and communities. And we'll also discuss the ethical considerations of game design and content, including the responsibilities of developers and the impact of gaming on societal norms. Finally, we'll explore how religion and spirituality are represented in games, examining the ways in which games can reflect, challenge, or reinforce religious beliefs and practices.

**REQUIRED**

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOtCBCnDG8g](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOtCBCnDG8g>)

In the talk, Madelyn Fried, a college freshman at the time of this recording, discusses the importance of civics education, noting a significant decline in its instruction across U.S. schools. ### Military Historian breaks down Call of Duty: Modern Warfare (2019)

**REQUIRED**

Article | ~9 min

The discussion highlights the game's accurate depiction of weaponry, character models, and sound effects, praising its attention to detail in areas like night vision technology. In this academic article, Frank Bosman outlines a comprehensive framework for analyzing the intersection of theology and digital media. He first establishes a nuanced definition of video games as digital, playable, and narrative texts, bridging the traditional gap between game mechanics and storytelling. To organize how faith appears in these spaces, Bosman identifies five distinct levels of religious engagement, ranging from explicit material content and external references to deep existential reflection and player-led rituals. Finally, he advocates for a four-step research methodology that requires scholars to combine immersive personal gameplay with a thorough investigation of developer intent and community reception.

### Video Games and Youth Violence: A Prospective Analysis in Adolescents

**EXPLORE FURTHER**

A study investigating the link between violent video game exposure and youth aggression found no significant long-term causal relationship. Instead, depressive symptoms emerged as a strong predictor of aggressive and violent behavior in adolescents, particularly when combined with antisocial traits. While acknowledging limitations such as a non-random, Hispanic-majority sample, the research suggests that fears regarding video games as a primary cause of youth violence may be unfounded, advocating for more conservative interpretations of such research findings.
