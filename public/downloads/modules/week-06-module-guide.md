# INT/AME 264 / Module Guide

Week 06 / Gaming's Impact on People and Society

## Games, Society, Religion, War, and Ethics

Exploring the impact of video games on civic engagement and the portrayal of war and violence.

This week, we explore how video games influence civic engagement and discuss the representation of war and violence in gaming. We will analyze the social and political implications of these themes and their effects on players and communities. And we'll also discuss the ethical considerations of game design and content, including the responsibilities of developers and the impact of gaming on societal norms. Finally, we'll explore how religion and spirituality are represented in games, examining the ways in which games can reflect, challenge, or reinforce religious beliefs and practices.
