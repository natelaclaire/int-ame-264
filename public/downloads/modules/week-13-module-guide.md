### Three States and a Plan: The A.I. of F.E.A.R. by Jeff Orkin

**EXPLORE FURTHER**

Academic Paper

[https://www.gamedevs.org/uploads/three-states-plan-ai-of-fear.pdf](<https://www.gamedevs.org/uploads/three-states-plan-ai-of-fear.pdf>)

This paper introduces the Goal-Oriented Action Planning (GOAP) system implemented in the game F.E.A.R., contrasting it with traditional Finite State Machines (FSMs) for AI behavior. While FSMs dictate precise actions, GOAP allows AI to reason and decide action sequences to achieve goals, significantly reducing development complexity and enabling more dynamic, believable character responses. The paper highlights three key benefits: decoupling goals from specific actions, allowing diverse characters to achieve similar goals uniquely; facilitating the layering of simple behaviors to create complex emergent tactics; and empowering dynamic problem-solving through real-time re-planning. Although squad behaviors were less formally implemented, the GOAP system for individual AI proved crucial for creating F.E.A.R.'s acclaimed combat experience. There is also a [conference talk based on this paper](https://gdcvault.com/play/1013282/Three-States-and-a-Plan) in case you prefer to listen to that.
