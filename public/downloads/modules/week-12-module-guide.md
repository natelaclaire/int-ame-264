# INT/AME 264 / Module Guide

Week 12 / Communities, Creativity, and Player Culture

## Gaming Communities and Tabletop Role-Playing Games

How gaming brings us together / divides us.

This week, we explore how gaming brings people together and how it can also create divisions within communities. We will discuss the social dynamics of gaming, including both positive and negative aspects. We'll also take a look at tabletop role-playing games (TTRPGs) and their impact on gaming culture, examining how these games foster creativity, collaboration, and storytelling within communities.

30 resources / 1 topic
